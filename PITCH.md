# Lesser: The Gmail of Decentralized Social Media

## Executive Summary

Lesser is building the first truly accessible decentralized social media platform by combining the open ActivityPub protocol with a revolutionary serverless architecture. We offer users their own professional social media presence (@you@yourdomain.com) for $9.99/month, with infrastructure costs of just $0.25 per user - enabling 90%+ gross margins.

**500 million users are looking for Twitter alternatives, but:**

- **Mastodon is too complex** - Users must choose servers, understand federation, manage technical details
- **Hosting is expensive** - Current solutions cost $50-200/month for basic instances
- **Setup is difficult** - Hours of configuration vs. minutes on centralized platforms

The market wants decentralized social media. The technology exists. But the user experience is killing adoption.
