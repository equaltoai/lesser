# Lesser: When Vision Meets Velocity

## The Moment of Clarity

For years, I'd been carrying a vision. It started when I discovered WebAuthn while researching distributed social networking. Then came [Solid](https://solidproject.org/), with Tim Berners-Lee's radical idea: users should own their data, not platforms.

As I built PayTheory's serverless infrastructure - processing millions in payments for pennies - I kept thinking: "Social media is doing this all wrong."

Mastodon proved federation could work. But it was still too expensive, too complex, too tied to old architectures. The vision crystallized:

- **Authentication without passwords** (WebAuthn from day one)
- **Data users actually own** (Solid's philosophy realized)  
- **Federation that works** (ActivityPub done right)
- **Costs that make sense** (Serverless, scaling to zero)
- **AI as enhancement** (Not gimmick, but core feature)

I knew exactly what to build. I just needed velocity.
