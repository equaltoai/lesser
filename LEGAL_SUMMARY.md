# Legal Documents Summary for Lesser

This document summarizes the key points from our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Always refer to the full documents for complete information.

## Privacy Policy Highlights

### 🔍 What Makes Lesser's Privacy Different

1. **Cost Transparency**: We show you exactly how much it costs to process your data (typically $0.00001-0.0001 per operation)

2. **Federation by Design**: Your public posts are copied to servers worldwide - this is permanent and irreversible

3. **Community Moderation**: Your content may be reviewed by trusted community members, not just algorithms

4. **AI Transparency**: We tell you when AI analyzes your content and for what purpose

### 📊 Data We Collect

- **Account Info**: Username, email, profile data
- **Content**: Posts, media, community notes, votes
- **Social Graph**: Follows, blocks, trust relationships
- **Metadata**: Timestamps, IP addresses, cost metrics
- **Federation Data**: Interactions with other servers

### 🤖 How We Use AI

- **AWS Comprehend**: Text analysis for spam/toxicity
- **AWS Rekognition**: Image moderation
- **AWS Bedrock**: AI content detection, semantic search
- **No Training**: We don't train AI on your data
- **Opt-Out Available**: You can request human-only moderation

### 🌐 Data Sharing

- **Federation**: Public content shared with ActivityPub network
- **AWS Services**: Processing through various AWS services
- **Community Moderators**: Flagged content visible to reviewers
- **Legal Compliance**: When required by law

### ✅ Your Rights

- Export all your data anytime
- Delete your account (but federated content remains)
- Control visibility settings
- Opt out of AI analysis
- Access cost metrics
- Transfer reputation scores

## Unique Features Explained

### Cost Transparency
Every database read, API call, and computation has a real AWS cost. We show you these costs because:
- It's honest
- It prevents abuse
- It helps you understand infrastructure
- It aligns incentives

### Reactive Moderation Mesh
Instead of top-down moderation:
- Community members review content
- Multiple reviewers create consensus
- Trust scores weight opinions
- Decisions are transparent
- Appeals are always possible

### Trust Graph
Your relationships affect the platform:
- Trust is directional and categorical
- Trust affects moderation weight
- Trust scores are portable
- Building trust takes time
- Trust can be lost quickly

### Community Notes
Like Twitter/X but federated:
- Add context to any public post
- Notes are voted on by community
- Helpful notes gain visibility
- Authors build reputation
- Facts must be sourced

### Portable Reputation
Your reputation belongs to you:
- Cryptographically signed scores
- Export and import anywhere
- Based on multiple factors
- Affects various features
- Cannot be faked
